HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. Every number explained.

  1. Pick a city. Use the map buttons at the top of the page. Tulsa has bundled 2027 prices. Omaha and Columbus have weather only, so their price stays Pending until you add prices in the Market prices step.
  2. Start from the template. The planner opens on a small fabricator with every value filled in, credit included. The note under each field says what it is and how the model uses it. Review the template offer ↗
  3. Change one value. Edit the margin, weather, a cost or a credit amount. The “What your changes do” table shows the effect on price, cash and credit next to the template. Reset to the template at any time. Open the steps ↗

A planning tool on estimated data, not a firm quote. Calculations run on your device, and refreshing the page clears your entries.
